Your visit

## What happens when you arrive

1. ### Assessment

   Tell us where it hurts and how it started. Bring any X-rays, scans, reports or prescriptions you have.
2. ### Treatment

   You receive hands-on treatment and guided exercises, matched to your condition.
3. ### Home plan and follow-up

   You leave with exercises to practise at home and the date of your next session.
